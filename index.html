How it works

Four steps from your phone or computer.

  1. Create your account

    Fill in your details and address. Staff review your registration to confirm you live in the barangay.

  2. Request a document

    Pick the document, state your purpose, and upload payment proof if a fee applies.

  3. Track every step

    Use your tracking number to see whether it is pending, processing, ready, or done.

  4. Collect it

    You get a notification when the document is ready for release at the barangay office.

About Barangay

This portal is the barangay's official channel for resident registration, document requests, concerns, and community announcements. It replaces repeated visits and paper follow-ups with one record you can check at any time.

Registration is verified by barangay staff. A verified mobile number or email alone does not prove residency.

Common questions

Who can register?

Residents of the barangay. Your registration is reviewed by staff, and you can use document services once it is approved.

How is my residency verified?

Staff check the details and supporting documents you submit against barangay records. They may ask you to correct or add information.

How much does a document cost and how do I pay?

The fee and accepted payment methods are shown on each service before you submit. Staff verify your proof of payment before processing continues. Never share bank passwords, PINs, or OTPs with anyone.

How long does it take?

Each service shows its usual processing time. You can follow progress with your tracking number at any time.

Need help? Visit or contact the barangay office.

Staff can assist with registration, payments, and any request you cannot complete online.
